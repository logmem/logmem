    Make it yours
    Name your space.
    A space is one walled-off memory: one team, one client, or one project. Agents connected to it can’t read any other space.
    
      
    
        
    
        
    Lowercase letters, numbers, - and _. Keys go under lm:…:*.
    
      
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    Plug in
    Connect your Redis.
    LogMem stores everything in a Redis you control. The test sends three read-only commands: PING, then TYPE and EXISTS on a random key that doesn’t exist. It writes nothing.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
        
    
      
    
      
    TLS (rediss://) on a public host, port 6379 or 6380.
    
      
    
      
    
      
    Your URL and token go to /api/onboarding/test once, are used for those three commands, and are dropped. This page doesn’t save them: they go into your agent’s own config in the next steps. Private and internal addresses are refused.
