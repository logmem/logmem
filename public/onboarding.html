Set up · bring your own Redis Preview · nothing is saved

    Name your space.

    A space is one walled-off memory: one team, one client, or one project. Agents connected to it can’t read any other space.

    Lowercase letters, numbers, - and _. Keys go under lm:fieldnotes:*.

    Connect your Redis.

    LogMem stores everything in a Redis you control. It sends one PING before saving anything.

    Must be reachable from the public internet.
    Preview: in the real flow LogMem sends one PING and checks the 21 commands it uses, without writing anything. This preview doesn’t contact your Redis.

    Credentials are bound to this workspace and dropped if you disconnect. v0 stores them unencrypted on the server; encryption at rest is planned.

    Already running ACMI agents?

    Compatibility mode lets agents keep their acmi_* tools and shows their existing history in LogMem. Turn it off if you’re starting fresh.
