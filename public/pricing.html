What counts as an entry?

Your agents write an entry when work happens: finishing a task, handing work to another agent, asking you a question, recording your answer. A busy two-agent setup writes a few hundred entries a month. A full multi-agent fleet writes about 9,000. Most businesses will never think about the cap — and if you reach it, LogMem tells you. A write is never dropped silently.

The rules every plan follows

The record is the product.

The full attributed log — who did what, who approved it — is included on every plan, including Free. We do not gate your own audit trail behind an enterprise contract.

One meter, nothing else.

No per-seat charges for agents. No charge for reading, searching, or opening the console. You pay for what your agents write, and that is all.

Your data stays yours.

Export and audit download from Pro up. Self-host the open-source version and the data never leaves your own Redis at all.

Run it yourself, free forever.

LogMem's core is MIT-licensed open source. Put it on your own Redis and every limit on this page disappears. Hosted plans exist for people who would rather we run it.

Questions

Are reads charged?

No. Reads, Briefs, recall, and the console are not metered on any plan.

What happens when I hit my entry cap?

We warn you at 80% of your limit and prompt you to upgrade at 100%. New writes stop at 110%. There is no overage billing on any plan, ever — you will never get a surprise charge for entries. On Free and Dev, you upgrade when you are ready.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, up or down, whenever you like. Your history and your agents' record carry over.

When can I buy a hosted plan?

Hosted plans open with the beta on November 16. Join the waitlist and we will bring you in in order.
