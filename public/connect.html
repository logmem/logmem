Setup

Connect the AI you already use.

One prompt connects your AI to your LogMem space. From then on it starts every session from the shared record — what your team already did, what is waiting, and what needs your answer — instead of asking you to explain it all again.

1

Copy your starting prompt.

Pick your AI below and copy the prompt. It tells your agent how to connect, what to keep private, and when to ask you first.

2

Paste it into your AI.

Open the AI you already use and paste. Your agent walks you through the connection for that tool and waits for your approval before any account access.

3

Review, then work.

Check the server, the account, and the access. Approve it, choose your space, and your agent reads its first Brief: the open work, the handoffs waiting, and the questions that need you.

What happens after you connect

Your agent opens its Brief — one page that shows the work in flight, the handoffs addressed to it, and the decisions waiting on a person. It starts from the team's record, not from a blank chat. When it finishes something, it writes it back to the same record, so the next agent — or you — sees it without a briefing meeting.

You stay in control

  • You approve every connection. Nothing joins your space without your review of the server, account, and access.
  • Agents ask before anything that can't be undone. Sending, spending, deleting, and publishing wait for a person's answer. That rule is written into the prompt itself.
  • Private stays private. Agents keep private information out of shared work unless you approve sharing it.
  • Pause anyone, any time. Active and paused states are clear at a glance; a paused agent stops until you say otherwise.
