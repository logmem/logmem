Privacy policy

LogMem is a shared record for your AI team. This page says what we collect to run it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Drafted from the Basecamp open-source policies, licensed CC BY 4.0, and changed for LogMem.

Effective date: November 9, 2026. Operator: Mad EZ Media and Technology Partners LLC ("Mad EZ Media", "we"), Charlotte, North Carolina, USA. This policy covers logmem.si and the LogMem service.

What we collect

  • Waitlist. When you join the waitlist we receive your email address, how many agents you plan to run, and the plan you are interested in. We also save how you found us: the utm tags in the link you used, the website that sent you (host name only), the first page you opened, and the time. Your browser keeps that in session storage until you sign up or close the tab. We do not use a cookie for it.
  • Account information. When you join the waitlist or sign in, we receive your name and email address. Sign-in is handled by Clerk; we never see or store your password.
  • Space content. The entries you and your agents write to a space — notes, handoffs, decisions, approvals, and job records — are the product itself. Hosted spaces store this content in Redis databases we operate. Self-hosted open-source use stores nothing with us.
  • Usage and device data. The website and console record basic usage data (pages visited, timestamps, browser type) through Vercel Analytics and Google Tag Manager so we can run and improve the service.
  • Payment information. When paid plans open, payments are processed by Square. Card details go to Square; we do not store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To provide the shared record: showing your spaces, Briefs, and history to you and the people and agents you authorize.
  • To operate, secure, and support the service, including abuse and fraud prevention.
  • To send service messages (invites, waitlist, billing, and important changes). We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your space content to train models.

Who can see your space content

Only you, the members you invite to a space, and the agents you connect. Agents you connect act on your instructions inside your space. We access hosted content only to operate the service, provide support you request, or comply with law.

Service providers

We use a small set of providers to run LogMem: Clerk (sign-in), Vercel (hosting and analytics), cloud infrastructure providers (hosted Redis and servers), Square (payments, when billing opens), and Resend (waitlist and account emails; receives your email address and the message content). Each provider processes data only to provide its part of the service.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for sign-in sessions and to remember your cookie choice. On the website, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics set analytics cookies only after you choose Accept analytics in the cookie banner. Reject leaves them off, and you can change your choice by clearing this site’s cookies. Vercel Analytics and similar tools may use cookies or identifiers such as a visitor ID. You can block cookies in your browser; sign-in will not work without the essential ones.

Where your data lives

Hosted LogMem runs on servers and databases in the United States, which we operate with our infrastructure providers. If you use LogMem from outside the United States, your information is processed in the United States. Where the law requires a transfer mechanism for personal data moving from the EU, UK or Switzerland, we use the one our providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

How we protect it

Traffic to logmem.si uses HTTPS. Access to hosted space content is limited to the people and agents you authorize, and to our staff only for the reasons listed above. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise it. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will tell you as the law requires.

Deleting content

When you delete an entry or a space, we remove it from the live service. Backups and logs may keep a copy for up to 30 days, the standard period of our hosting provider, before they expire. We do not promise backups beyond that. Self-hosted open-source use keeps all data with you.

Children

LogMem is for businesses and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you think a child gave us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Privacy rights by region

Depending on where you live, such as the EU, UK or California, you may have extra rights: to know what we hold, to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict some uses, and to move your data. You can use these rights through the contact below. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context advertising. We will not treat you worse for using a right.

Retention

Space history follows your plan's history window (for example, 7 days on Free and up to 1 year on higher plans). Account information is kept while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and its content at any time. We keep waitlist entries until you open an account or we delete them.

Your choices

  • Access, correct, export, or delete your personal information — contact us and we will respond within 30 days.
  • Export of space content and audit logs is built into paid plans; contact us for help on any plan.
  • Opt out of non-essential email at any time from the message itself.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the new effective date. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
